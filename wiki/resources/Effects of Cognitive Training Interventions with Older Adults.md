# Effects of Cognitive Training Interventions with Older Adults: A Randomized Controlled Trial

- **论文**: Ball, Berch, Helmers, ... Willis (2002). Effects of cognitive training interventions with older adults: a randomized controlled trial. *JAMA*, 288(18), 2271–2281（ACTIVE 试验首报）。
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12425704/
- **权威等级**: primary（大样本随机对照试验）
- **检索日期**: 2026-08-05；**摄入日期**: 2026-08-05
- **独立性**: 老年人可训练性权威锚点
- **证据状态**: 摘要引文逐字核验（PubMed）

## 摘要要点（逐字）

> "Each intervention improved the targeted cognitive ability compared with baseline, durable to 2 years (P<.001 for all). ... No training effects on everyday functioning were detected at 2 years."

> "Training effects were of a magnitude equivalent to the amount of decline expected in elderly persons without dementia over 7- to 14-year intervals."

（locator: abstract, Results / Conclusions）

## 方法与结论

- ACTIVE：2,832 名 65–94 岁社区老人；记忆/推理/处理速度三种训练 vs 对照；2 年随访。
- **目标能力提升且持续 2 年；日常生活功能无迁移**；效应量级 ≈ 7–14 年自然衰退量。
- 10 年随访（NIA 2014）：推理与处理速度收益保留、记忆训练效应消失、功能测验无差异——见 [[成人脑可塑性]]。
