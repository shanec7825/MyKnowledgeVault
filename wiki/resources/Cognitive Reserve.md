# Cognitive Reserve

- **论文**: Stern, Y. (2009). Cognitive reserve. *Neuropsychologia*, 47(10), 2015–2028.
- **URL**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2739591/（DOI 10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2009.03.004）
- **权威等级**: primary（认知储备理论奠基综述）

## 引言要点（逐字）

> "The concept of reserve has been proposed to account for the disjunction between the degree of brain damage or pathology and its clinical manifestations."

（locator: Introduction）

> "Cognitive reserve (CR) postulates that individual differences in the cognitive processes or neural networks underlying task performance allow some people to cope better than others with brain damage."

（locator: Introduction）

## 主动模型与生活经历

- 与被动模型（脑储备/容量阈值）不同，CR 是**主动模型**：大脑调用既有认知过程或代偿性过程应对损伤（Stern 2002 谱系）。
- 教育、职业暴露、休闲活动等生活经历与**痴呆风险降低**及**正常老化中记忆衰退减慢**相关——"终身可塑积累"的流行病学支柱，见 [[神经可塑性 认知科学理论]]。
