# Repeated Testing Produces Superior Transfer of Learning Relative to Repeated Studying

- **论文**: Butler, A. C. (2010). Repeated testing produces superior transfer of learning relative to repeated studying. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition*, 36(5), 1118–1133.
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1037/a0019902
- **权威等级**: primary（4 项实验）
- **检索日期**: 2026-08-06；**摄入日期**: 2026-08-06
- **独立性**: 提取练习促进迁移的关键实验锚点
- **证据状态**: 结果经搜索引擎/二级来源核验

## 核心发现

> "Repeated testing produced superior retention and transfer relative to repeated studying on the final test across all experiments."

- 4 项实验一致：反复测试 > 反复学习——对事实性和概念性信息均适用。
- Experiment 3 特别证明了向新知识领域的远迁移。
- 检索**成功**是关键机制；可变测试（变换问法）未优于相同问题重复测试——与"编码变异性假说"不符。

## 边界

Tran, Rohrer & Pashler (2015) 发现提取练习对演绎推理无迁移收益。见 [[认知迁移能力]]。
