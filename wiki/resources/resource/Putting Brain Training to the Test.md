# Putting Brain Training to the Test

- **论文**: Owen, Hampshire, Grahn, Stenton, Dajani, Burns, Howard & Ballard (2010). Putting brain training to the test. *Nature*, 465, 775–778.
- **URL**: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09042
- **权威等级**: primary（同行评议原始研究）
- **检索日期**: 2026-08-05；**摄入日期**: 2026-08-05
- **独立性**: 脑力训练"无迁移"阵营的权威 RCT 锚点
- **证据状态**: 摘要引文逐字核验；正文付费墙

## 摘要要点（逐字）

> "Although improvements were observed in every one of the cognitive tasks that were trained, no evidence was found for transfer effects to untrained tasks, even when those tasks were cognitively closely related."

（locator: abstract）

## 方法与结论

- **11,430 名**参与者，六周在线随机对照训练：推理、记忆、计划、视空间、注意五个任务。
- 全部训练任务均进步 → **对未训练任务（即使认知相近）零迁移**。
- 与 [[Improving Fluid Intelligence with Training on Working Memory]]（Jaeggi 2008）的迁移主张直接形成对照；评估见 [[神经可塑性 认知科学理论]]（训练迁移之争，`contested`）。
