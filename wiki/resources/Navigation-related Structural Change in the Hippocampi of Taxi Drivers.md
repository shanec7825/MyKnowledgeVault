# Navigation-Related Structural Change in the Hippocampi of Taxi Drivers

- **论文**: Maguire, Gadian, Johnsrude, Good, Ashburner, Frackowiak & Frith (2000). Navigation-related structural change in the hippocampi of taxi drivers. *PNAS*, 97(8), 4398–4403.
- **URL**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10716738/
- **权威等级**: primary（同行评议原始研究）
- **检索日期**: 2026-08-05；**摄入日期**: 2026-08-05
- **独立性**: 成人结构性可塑性经典锚点
- **证据状态**: 摘要引文逐字核验（PubMed）

## 摘要要点（逐字）

> "Hippocampal volume correlated with the amount of time spent as a taxi driver (positively in the posterior and negatively in the anterior hippocampus). It seems that there is a capacity for local plastic change in the structure of the healthy adult human brain in response to environmental demands."

（locator: abstract）

## 方法与结论

- 伦敦出租车司机（n=16，数年高强度空间导航训练）vs 对照（n=16，非导航职业），结构性 MRI。
- **后海马体积显著更大，且与驾驶年限正相关**（前海马负相关）。
- 横断面设计：相关而非因果；但作为"健康成人大脑对环境需求作出局部结构改变"的直接证据被广泛引用（见 [[成人脑可塑性]]）。
