# Improving Fluid Intelligence with Training on Working Memory

- **论文**: Jaeggi, Buschkuehl, Jonides & Perrig (2008). Improving fluid intelligence with training on working memory. *PNAS*, 105(19), 6829–6833.
- **URL**: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0801268105（PMID 18443283）
- **权威等级**: primary（同行评议原始研究）
- **检索日期**: 2026-08-05；**摄入日期**: 2026-08-05
- **独立性**: "训练可提升流体智力"主张的原始阳性锚点
- **证据状态**: 摘要引文逐字核验（Europe PMC/NCBI）

## 摘要要点（逐字）

> "we demonstrate that the extent of gain in intelligence critically depends on the amount of training: the more training, the more improvement in Gf. That is, the training effect is dosage-dependent."

（locator: abstract）

## 方法与主张

- 双 n-back 工作记忆训练（与瑞文等测验完全不同的任务）→ 流体智力（Gf）提升，且剂量依赖。
- 该主张与 [[Putting Brain Training to the Test]]（Owen 2010）的零迁移及 [[No Evidence of Intelligence Improvement after Working Memory Training]]（Redick 2013）的失败复现**直接矛盾**；评估：`contested`（见 [[神经可塑性 认知科学理论]]）。
