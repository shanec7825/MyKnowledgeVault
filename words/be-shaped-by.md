# be shaped by

**Meaning:** be influenced or formed by something; does not imply one exclusive cause.

**Pattern:** X **is shaped by** Y; plural: X **are shaped by** Y.

**2026-10-20 context:** Autumn leaf colors **are shaped by** tree species, sunlight, moisture, and temperature. Seasonal changes in chlorophyll help explain the mechanism.

**Retrieval:** “学生的学习体验受到反馈影响” → A student's learning experience ______ feedback.

**Transfer:** The quality of an AI project can be shaped by team communication as well as model performance.
