# get caught up in

**Meaning:** become absorbed in something and lose sight of a broader goal.

**Pattern:** get caught up in + noun / -ing.

**Today's context:** Rather than get caught up in worrying about a score, attend to the next useful action.

**Chinese → English retrieval:** “我有时会陷入琐碎的细节。” → I sometimes ______ minor details.

**Transfer:** While debugging, I can get caught up in an error message and forget the program's purpose.
