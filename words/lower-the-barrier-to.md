- **核心意义**：让某件事更容易开始或参与。
- **关系**：difficulty at the beginning → easier access or participation.
- **搭配**：lower the barrier to entry; lower the barrier to learning; lower the barrier to participation.
- **迁移例句**：Clear instructions can lower the barrier to learning a new programming framework.
- **反向提取**：“降低参加一个活动的门槛” → ______.
