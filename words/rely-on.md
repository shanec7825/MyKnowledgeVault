# rely on

**Meaning:** depend on something.

**Context:** Whales rely on sound for communication.

**Retrieval:** 依靠 → ______

**Transfer:** I rely on feedback when learning English.

Related: [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-10-English-Whale-Songs-and-Ocean-Noise]]

**2026-10-19 · Offline Knowledge:** Students rely on a stable power supply even when a digital library is offline. Retrieve from “依赖稳定供电” → “rely on stable electricity.” Transfer: language learners rely on feedback, not only exposure.
