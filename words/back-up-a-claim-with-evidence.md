# back up a claim with evidence

**Meaning:** support a statement with observations, examples or data. A claim is not proven merely because it sounds plausible.

**Context:** Back up your interpretation with evidence before treating it as a conclusion.

**Retrieval:** “用证据支持判断” → ______.

**Transfer:** How would you back up a claim that an AI tutor helps students learn?
