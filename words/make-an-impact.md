# make an impact

**Meaning:** Produce a noticeable or meaningful effect. This expression does **not** by itself establish causality in research.

**2026-10-29 · Beyond the Box Score:** A timely screen can make an impact even when the player does not score.

**Pattern:** make an impact **on** + person/team/outcome; **IM**-pact (noun stress on first syllable).

**Retrieval (cover the English):** 产生影响 → make an impact.

**Transfer:** A clear bug report can make an impact on a project's reliability.

**Contrast:** [[words/play-a-role-in|play a role in]] describes contribution to a process; *make an impact* highlights a meaningful effect.
