# in turn

**Meaning:** As a consequence; or reciprocally, in the next step of a relationship.

**2026-10-28 · Roots Below Ground:** Rhizobia provide usable nitrogen; the plant, in turn, supplies sugars.

**Pattern:** X happens; this, in turn, affects Y. / X provides A; Y, in turn, provides B.

**Retrieval (cover the English):** 反过来 / 进而 → in turn

**Transfer:** Better tests reveal bugs; this, in turn, improves software reliability.
