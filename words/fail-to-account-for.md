# fail to account for

**Core relation:** An explanation, model or plan overlooks an important condition or factor.

**Today's context:** The vision-language model may **fail to account for** the word *not* in an image-search request.

**Retrieval (Chinese → English):** “模型没有考虑否定信息。” → The model ______ negation.

**Sentence pattern:** X fails to account for Y; X failed to account for Y when Z.

**Transfer:** A study of university learning habits might fail to account for students' sleep schedules. Make your own sentence about debugging.

**Don't confuse with:** *account for* = explain or consider; *fail to account for* = not include a relevant factor.
