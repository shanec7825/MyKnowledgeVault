# weigh X against Y

**Meaning:** compare competing factors before making a decision.

**Pattern:** weigh + noun A + against + noun B.

**Today's context:** Universities should weigh service costs against possible benefits before opening a repair program.

**Chinese → English retrieval:** “权衡价格与可靠性” → ______ price ______ reliability.

**Transfer:** Engineers weigh model accuracy against inference cost.
