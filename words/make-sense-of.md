- **词性/类型**：固定表达
- **核心意义**：把复杂、零散或令人困惑的信息组织起来，从而理解它。
- **核心关系**：complex / confusing information → organize → understand
- **常见搭配**：make sense of data · make sense of an article · make sense of what happened
- **主动调用情境**：局部信息都认识，但需要把它们整合成整体模型。

> I understand the individual sentences, but I still need to make sense of the article as a whole.
