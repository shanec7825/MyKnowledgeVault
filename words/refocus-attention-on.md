# refocus attention on

**Meaning:** deliberately direct attention back to a task or object.

**Pattern:** refocus (your) attention on + noun / -ing.

**Today's context:** A short cue can help a player refocus attention on the next action.

**Chinese → English retrieval:** “把注意力重新集中到题目的条件。” → ______ the question's conditions.

**Transfer:** When reading a research article, refocus attention on the author's central claim.
