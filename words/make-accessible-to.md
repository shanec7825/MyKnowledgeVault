# make ... accessible to ...

**Meaning:** design or adapt something so a group of people can use, reach, or understand it.

**Pattern:** *make + something + accessible to + someone* (not *accessible for* in this pattern).

**2026-10-26 · AI and Accessibility:** Captions can **make** video lessons **accessible to** students who cannot hear the audio.

**Retrieval:** “让技术文档对新手更容易获取” → make technical documentation accessible to beginners.

**Transfer:** A plain-language error message can make a programming tool accessible to more first-time users.
