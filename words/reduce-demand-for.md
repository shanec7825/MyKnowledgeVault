# reduce demand for

**Meaning:** decrease how much of a product, service or resource people want or need. A predicted decrease is not necessarily a measured decrease.

**Pattern:** Bike-sharing may reduce demand for fuel-based travel.

**Chinese → English:** “一种新的出行方式可能减少对燃油的需求。”先独立组句，再打开答案。

**New context:** How could video meetings reduce demand for business travel?
