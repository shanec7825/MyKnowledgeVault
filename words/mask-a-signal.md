# mask a signal

**Meaning:** make an acoustic signal harder to hear because another sound overlaps it.

**Today's context:** Engine noise may mask a quieter animal call.

**Retrieval:** “较强的声音盖过了较弱的声音” → ______.

**Transfer:** In a noisy café, background music may mask part of a conversation.
