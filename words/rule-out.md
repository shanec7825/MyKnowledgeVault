# rule out

**Core relation:** Exclude a possibility after checking relevant evidence; do not confuse with merely disliking a possibility.

**Today's context:** A smooth-looking image cannot, by itself, **rule out** the possibility of AI generation.

**Retrieval (Chinese → English):** “我们还不能排除这个解释。” → We cannot ______ this explanation yet.

**Sentence patterns:** rule out a possibility; cannot rule out X; rule out X by checking Y.

**Transfer:** What test would help you rule out a bug in your data-processing code? Answer in one original sentence.

**Evidence caution:** Failure to find evidence is not always enough to rule out a claim.
