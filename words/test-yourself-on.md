# test yourself on ...

**Meaning:** check your own understanding or recall of a particular topic, ideally without seeing the answer first.

**Pattern:** test yourself **on** + topic; test yourself **with** + questions.

**English → meaning:** Test yourself on the article's structure before reopening the page. → 重新打开网页前，先自测是否能回忆文章结构。

**Meaning → English (retrieve first):** “不要看笔记，先就数据结构自测。” → ___ yourself ___ data structures before looking at your notes.

**Transfer:** Create one question about a new AI concept; attempt the answer from memory, then check.
