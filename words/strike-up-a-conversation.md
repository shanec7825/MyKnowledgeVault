# strike up a conversation

**Meaning:** begin a friendly, usually informal conversation with someone.

**Pattern:** strike up a conversation **with** a person **about** a topic.

**2026-10-21 context:** Before a university lecture, you could strike up a conversation with a classmate about a shared project—when they seem open to chatting.

**Recognition → retrieval:** “和同学主动聊起来” → ___ ___ a conversation with a classmate.

**Transfer:** In an AI research club, what shared question could help you strike up a conversation without interrupting someone's work?

**Contrast:** *keep a conversation going* means continue one; *strike up* means start it.
