# go back to (an origin)

**Active meaning:** to have begun at an earlier time or in an earlier event; **可追溯到，起源于**. This is the historical-origin sense, not simply “return to a place.”

**Pattern:** a custom / tradition / practice **goes back to** + a period or event.

**Today's exact source context:** VOA's *Grammar and Halloween* introduces a possible Celtic connection with **“Some people believe…”**, so the origin is presented as a belief, **not** settled historical proof.

**Retrieve before revealing:** “这个社团的传统可以追溯到很多年前。” → This club tradition ___ ___ ___ many years ago.

**Transfer (do not copy):** Say one sentence about a family custom or a software convention. If evidence is uncertain, start with **Some people believe…**.
