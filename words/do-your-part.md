# do your part

**Meaning:** Fulfil your share of a shared responsibility. It is about **responsibility**, not how visible the work is.

**2026-10-29 · Beyond the Box Score:** You can do your part by defending well, even if you rarely touch the ball.

**Pattern:** do your part **in** a project; do your part **to help** the team succeed. Stress **PART**.

**Retrieval (cover the English):** 尽自己的一份力 → do your part.

**Transfer:** A teammate who writes tests and documentation is doing their part in a software release.
