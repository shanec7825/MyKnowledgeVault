# be associated with ...

**Meaning:** be statistically or observationally linked to; this alone does **not** prove causation.

**Pattern:** X is associated with Y. Avoid rewriting it as X causes Y without stronger evidence.

**Meaning → English:** “睡眠时间与考试成绩有关联。” → Sleep duration was ___ ___ exam results.

**Original context:** In the small classroom study, more sleep was associated with better exam performance; participation was not randomly assigned.

**Transfer:** In a programming project, more frequent commits may be associated with fewer integration problems, but team practices could explain both.

**Pronunciation:** as-**SO**-ci-at-ed; emphasize the SO syllable.
