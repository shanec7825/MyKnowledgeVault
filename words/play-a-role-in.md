# play a role in

**Meaning:** Contribute to a process or outcome, without claiming it is the only cause.

**2026-10-28 · Roots Below Ground:** Roots play a role in reducing soil erosion.

**Pattern:** play a role in + noun / verb-ing.

**Retrieval (cover the English):** 发挥作用 → play a role in ...

**Transfer:** Clear documentation plays a role in successful team projects.
