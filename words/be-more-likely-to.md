# be more likely to

**Meaning:** have a higher probability of doing something; it does **not** mean *always*.

**Pattern:** subject + be + more likely to + base verb (+ than ...).

**Example:** Students who practice recalling a phrase may be more likely to remember it later, but the result depends on the task.

**Chinese → English retrieval:** “做过独立检索练习的人可能更容易发现错误。” → People who practice retrieval may be ___ ___ ___ spot errors.

**New-context transfer:** Compare two ways of learning a programming concept without claiming certainty.
