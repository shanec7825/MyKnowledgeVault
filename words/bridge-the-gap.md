# bridge the gap

**Meaning:** reduce a difference in access, skills, or opportunities; not necessarily eliminate it.

**2026-10-19 · Offline Knowledge:** Offline digital libraries can **bridge the gap** in educational access when internet connections are unreliable.

**Retrieval:** “弥合初学者与有经验者之间的差距” → “bridge the gap between beginners and experienced learners.”

**Transfer:** A student AI team can bridge the gap between theory and practice through small projects.
