# bridge the gap

**Meaning:** reduce a difference in access, skills, or opportunities; not necessarily eliminate it.

**2026-10-19 · Offline Knowledge:** Offline digital libraries can **bridge the gap** in educational access when internet connections are unreliable.

**Retrieval:** “弥合初学者与有经验者之间的差距” → “bridge the gap between beginners and experienced learners.”

**Transfer:** A student AI team can bridge the gap between theory and practice through small projects.

## 2026-10-25 · Libraries Beyond Books

A public library may **bridge the gap** in digital access by lending devices and offering learning support. This describes a possible role, not proof that the gap disappears.

**Retrieval:** “图书馆帮助缩小数字鸿沟” → A library can ___ the ___ in digital access.

**Transfer:** Peer mentoring can bridge the gap between reading about algorithms and implementing them.

## 2026-10-26 · AI and Accessibility

Accessible course design can **bridge the gap** between learners with different access needs, but it does not guarantee identical outcomes.

**Retrieval:** “缩小不同学生使用课程资料的差距” → bridge the gap in access to course materials.

**Transfer:** A beginner-friendly API tutorial can bridge the gap between knowing syntax and building an app.
