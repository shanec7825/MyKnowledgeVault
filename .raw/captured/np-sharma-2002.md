# A sensitive period for the development of the central auditory system in children with cochlear implants: implications for age of implantation

URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12476090/
Captured: 2026-08-05 (autoresearch)
Verbatim status: 见各段标注；未标注段为逐字捕获。

> Conclusions/abstract (PubMed, verbatim)

> Our data suggest that in the absence of normal stimulation there is a sensitive period of about 3.5 yr during which the human central auditory system remains maximally plastic. Plasticity remains in some, but not all children until approximately age 7. After age 7, plasticity is greatly reduced.
