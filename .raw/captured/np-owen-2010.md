# Putting brain training to the test

URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09042
Captured: 2026-08-05 (autoresearch)
Verbatim status: 见各段标注；未标注段为逐字捕获。

> abstract (Nature, verbatim)

> The central question is not whether performance on cognitive tests can be improved by training, but rather, whether those benefits transfer to other untrained tasks or lead to any general improvement in the level of cognitive functioning.

> Although improvements were observed in every one of the cognitive tasks that were trained, no evidence was found for transfer effects to untrained tasks, even when those tasks were cognitively closely related.
