# Human hippocampal neurogenesis persists throughout aging

URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29625071/
Captured: 2026-08-05 (autoresearch)
Verbatim status: 见各段标注；未标注段为逐字捕获。

> abstract (PubMed, verbatim)

> We found similar numbers of intermediate neural progenitors and thousands of immature neurons in the DG, comparable numbers of glia and mature granule neurons, and equivalent DG volume across ages.

> Thus, healthy older subjects without cognitive impairment, neuropsychiatric disease, or treatment display preserved neurogenesis. It is possible that ongoing hippocampal neurogenesis sustains human-specific cognitive function throughout life and that declines may be linked to compromised cognitive-emotional resilience.
