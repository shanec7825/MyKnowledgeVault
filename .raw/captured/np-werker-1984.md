# Cross-language speech perception: Evidence for perceptual reorganization during the first year of life

Captured: 2026-08-05 (autoresearch)
Verbatim status: 见各段标注；未标注段为逐字捕获。

> abstract (ScienceDirect first-page, verbatim)

> The results of these experiments replicate our original findings by showing that infants can discriminate nonnative speech contrasts without relevant experience, and that there is a decline in this ability during ontogeny. Furthermore, data from both cross-sectional and longitudinal studies shows that this decline occurs within the first year of life, and that it is a function of specific language experience.
