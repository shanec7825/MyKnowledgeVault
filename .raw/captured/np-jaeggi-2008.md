# Improving fluid intelligence with training on working memory

URL: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0801268105

> abstract (Europe PMC/PNAS, verbatim)

> Fluid intelligence (Gf) refers to the ability to reason and to solve new problems independently of previously acquired knowledge.

> Here, we present evidence for transfer from training on a demanding working memory task to measures of Gf. This transfer results even though the trained task is entirely different from the intelligence test itself.

> we demonstrate that the extent of gain in intelligence critically depends on the amount of training: the more training, the more improvement in Gf. That is, the training effect is dosage-dependent.
