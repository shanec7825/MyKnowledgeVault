> In general, these studies reveal that older adults can indeed benefit from training, albeit often at a slower rate than younger adults do (Baltes et al., 1989; Willis et al., 2006; Winocur et al., 2007).

> Participants did improve on the trained tasks and other measures of these processes. However, no significant transfer occurred between the trained and untrained cognitive processes (e.g., those individuals receiving memory training did not improve on speed of processing and vice versa).
