> abstract (PMC full text, verbatim)

> The developmental timing of critical periods for learning phonetic, lexical, and syntactic levels of language vary... the critical period for phonetic learning occurs prior to the end of the first year, whereas syntactic learning flourishes between 18 and 36 months of age. Vocabulary development 'explodes' at 18 months of age, but does not appear to be as restricted by age as other aspects of language learning—one can learn new vocabulary items at any age.
