Matching text for the SYNTHESIZED study summary

People often look peaceful while sleeping, but the brain may remain extremely active. Researchers investigated what happens to blood supply and cellular energy around rapid eye movement sleep. They reported a surprising difference between the two signals. Blood flow could increase even as a measure of available cellular energy decreased. One possibility is that active neurons use energy very quickly. Yet the observation and the proposed explanation are not identical. The article shows why scientists must separate what they measure from how they explain it.
