Matching text for the SYNTHESIZED study summary

Self-driving vehicles rely on complex systems that can make unexpected mistakes. People watching these systems may not understand why a decision was made or when an error is likely. MIT researchers developed CW-Net to represent aspects of a model's reasoning with understandable concepts. The approach aims to help people anticipate failures instead of simply trusting a confident-looking output. An explanation can support human judgment, but it is not the same as a safety guarantee. The important question is whether people can actually make better predictions from it.
