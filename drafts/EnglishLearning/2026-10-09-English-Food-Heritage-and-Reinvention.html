Speaking

Should traditions stay unchanged to remain authentic?

  1. What food or everyday practice reminds you of home?
  2. Why might people want to pass down that practice?
  3. What changes when a tradition moves to another country?
  4. Can a new version be respectful without being identical?
  5. Should universities preserve old teaching methods or make room for new ones?
