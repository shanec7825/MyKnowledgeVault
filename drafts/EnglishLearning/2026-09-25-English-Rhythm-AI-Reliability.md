# 2026-09-25 · Rhythm × AI Reliability

## Today's Focus

**连续语音的结构感 + 技术文章中的“严格要求”。**

## 1. Extensive Listening · 8 min

**Source:** [TED-Ed — A different way to visualize rhythm](https://ed.ted.com/lessons/a-different-way-to-visualize-rhythm-john-varney)

1. 第一遍不看字幕：What is being visualized? Why a circle?
2. 第二遍只找 **idea → example → explanation**。
3. transcript 只检查真正卡住的两处。
4. 用 **rhythm / circle / beats / pattern** 脱稿复述。

## 2. Intensive Reading · 9 min

**Source:** [MIT News — New method enables AI for safety-critical situations](https://news.mit.edu/2026/new-method-enables-ai-safety-critical-situations-0914)

### Article model

```text
PROBLEM
plausible / almost correct is not enough
        ↓
REQUIREMENT
hard constraints must be satisfied
        ↓
METHOD
more freedom during generation
+ enforce constraints on final output
        ↓
RESULT
better solutions while obeying requirements
```

## 3. Active Expressions

- **satisfy a requirement**
- **rather than**
- **without sacrificing X**

Retrieval:
> How can we improve speed without sacrificing ______?

## 4. Speaking

**When is “almost correct” not good enough?**

1. When is a small mistake acceptable?
2. When can a programming mistake cause a big problem?
3. Why might AI need hard constraints?
4. Where is flexibility useful during the process but strictness required at the end?

Keywords: **constraint · requirement · acceptable · reliable · trade-off**

## 5. Pronunciation / Rhythm

> We need to improve **SAFEty** / without sacrificing per**FOR**mance.

训练强弱和意群，不追求每个音节一样用力。

## Reflection

- Did I hear words but lose the structure?
- Could I explain **hard constraint** in my own English?
- Could I retrieve **without sacrificing** in a new context?
