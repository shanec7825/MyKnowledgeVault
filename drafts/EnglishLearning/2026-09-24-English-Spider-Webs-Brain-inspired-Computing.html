Matching text for the SYNTHESIZED study summary

A spider's web may look silent, but vibrations can carry important information. Scientists recorded patterns in the web and used computers to turn those patterns into audible sounds. This does not mean that the spider is speaking human language. The sounds offer a new way to study how spiders respond to their surroundings. Researchers hope that understanding the structure and signals of a web could also inspire engineering. The report illustrates how a mathematical model can reveal a world we cannot directly hear.
