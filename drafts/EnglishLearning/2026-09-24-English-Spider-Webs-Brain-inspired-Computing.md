# 2026-09-24 · Spider Webs × Brain-inspired Computing

← [[drafts/EnglishLearning/README|English Learning Hub]]

## Today's Focus

**听/读后的意义压缩 → 脱稿复述 → 换情境调用。**

## 1. Extensive Listening · 8 min

**Source:** [VOA Learning English — Listening to a Spider's Web](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/amp/listening-to-a-spider-s-web/5854255.html)

训练顺序：

1. 不看稿听，回答 *What is the story mainly about?*
2. 第二遍判断断点：vocabulary / sound recognition / chunking / retrieval。
3. 看 transcript 只修 1–2 个片段。
4. 关稿，用 4 个关键词复述 30 秒。

## 2. Intensive Reading · 9 min

**Source:** [Scientific American — Could brain-inspired computers run on the same amount of power as a human brain?](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/could-brain-inspired-computers-run-on-the-same-amount-of-power-as-a-human-brain/)

### Article model

```text
OBSERVATION
human brain ≈ 20 W
        ↓
CONTRAST
modern AI → much larger infrastructure
        ↓
QUESTION
Why such an efficiency gap?
        ↓
DIRECTION
neuromorphic / brain-inspired computing
```

阅读时问：**What is the writer doing here?**

## 3. Active Expressions

- **lead to** — cause a result.
- **a small fraction of** — only a limited part of something larger.
- **not by A, but by B** — contrast two approaches.

Retrieval:
> I want to improve my English not by ______, but by ______.

## 4. Speaking

**Should computers become more like brains?**

1. What makes the brain energy-efficient?
2. What differs between today's computers and brains?
3. What might brain-inspired computing improve?
4. Do computers actually need to imitate the brain?

Keywords: **energy-efficient · architecture · process information · imitate · trade-off**

## 5. Sound / Rhythm

Chunk:
> The HUMAN brain / can process a huge amount of INformation / while using surprisingly little ENergy.

重点：意群和信息重音，而不是逐词等重音。

3. **1 min** — 关闭文章复述 main idea。
4. **1 min** — 反向调用一个主动表达。
