Study summary transcript (generated by this learning package)

People who build technology are not always the people who decide how it will be used. The MIT article describes efforts to document organizing among technology workers. These stories include debates about workplace conditions, social responsibility, and power inside companies. Recording a movement can help others understand why workers chose to speak up. It does not mean that every engineer supported the same idea or policy. The report invites readers to ask who should have a say when a technical decision affects people beyond the development team.
