# 2026-10-04 · Worker Voice × Tech Responsibility

← [[drafts/EnglishLearning/README|English Learning Hub]]

## Today's Focus

昨天训练的是复杂系统中的 **specialization / interaction**；今天转到 **technology × society**，关注一个系统里的参与者如何争取影响决策。

今天维护的全文模型：

```text
ATTRACTION → CONCERN → COLLECTIVE ACTION → RESPONSE → DECLINE → QUESTION
```

核心训练仍然是：读懂一句之后，立刻问 **What changed in the story?**

## 1. Extensive Listening · 7 min

**Source:** [BBC Learning English — 6 Minute English: Why does heartbreak hurt so much?](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english_2026/ep-261001)

该节目发布于 2026-10-01，约 6 分钟，并提供 transcript / worksheet。

### Round 1 · no transcript

完整听一遍，只维护三个槽位：

```text
QUESTION → EXPLANATION → WHY IT MATTERS
```

听完后用 20–30 秒英语回答：

> Why can an emotional experience produce a physical feeling?

不要追求节目里的全部词汇，只重建因果链。

### Round 2 · diagnose one breakdown

打开 transcript，只定位第一次真正掉线的位置：

- sound recognition
- vocabulary
- chunking
- retention
- relationship

只修这一处，再从前一句重新听到后一句。目标不是“全文精听”，而是确认 **为什么模型在这里断了**。

### 30-second retrieval

关掉 transcript，用 4 个词复述节目。四个词必须由你自己选，因为“选择节点”本身就是压缩训练。

## 2. Intensive Reading · 11 min

**Source:** [MIT News — Documenting the tech worker movement](https://news.mit.edu/2026/documenting-tech-worker-movement-0918)

MIT News 于 2026-10-02 发布这篇文章，介绍 JS Tan 和 Clarissa Redwine 对过去十年科技行业员工组织行动的记录与分析。

### First pass

每读 2–3 段，只写一句：

> **The writer is now explaining ...**

禁止摘抄原句。

特别留意文章从“科技行业为什么吸引人”转向“为什么部分员工开始要求更多话语权”，再转向“为什么这种组织行动后来减弱”。

### Build the model yourself

先关闭文章，自己填空：

```text
TECH INDUSTRY
attractive career + belief in technology
        ↓
________
workers become concerned about decisions
        ↓
________
workers organize / protest / demand influence
        ↓
EMPLOYER RESPONSE
        ↓
________
movement loses momentum
        ↓
ANALYTICAL QUESTION
why did this change happen?
```

再对照你自己的阅读笔记，而不是重新逐句读全文。

### Framework

**CONTEXT → TENSION → ACTION → COUNTERACTION → OUTCOME → EXPLANATION**

注意：这是一个“过程随时间变化”的文章，不要硬套 Problem → Method → Result。

### Global-model check

用一句英语概括全文。如果你的句子只是描述某次具体事件，抽象层级可能太低；尝试再提升一级：

> This article is mainly about how ______ changed over time and why ______.

## 3. Active Expressions · 4 min

### [[have-a-say|have a say]]

核心不是普通的 “say something”，而是：

```text
a decision affects you
        ↓
you have influence / input
in that decision
```

Examples:
- Students should have a say in how a course is improved.
- Developers want to have a say in how a product is designed.

**Retrieval cue:** “这件事会影响我，所以我也应该能参与决定。”

> Team members should ______ in major design decisions.

### [[words/push-back-against|push back against]]

核心关系：

```text
pressure / policy / proposal
        ↓
you resist or challenge it
```

它比简单的 disagree 更强调 **主动抵制、反对或挑战**。

> Engineers may push back against a deadline they believe is unrealistic.

**Retrieval cue:** “不是只不同意，而是对某种压力或决定作出抵制。”

### Old expression retrieval

昨天的 [[complementary|complementary]]：

> Technical knowledge and social analysis can provide ______ perspectives on the same technology.

先说出来，再打开词汇笔记核对。

## 4. Sentence / Discourse Skill · 2 min

### within + time period = track change

文章讨论一个运动如何随时间改变。遇到：

> within several years ...

不要只处理成时间状语，而要提醒自己：

> **Compare the earlier state with the later state.**

自己造句：

> Within one semester, my English learning changed from ______ to ______.

这会强迫你把多个局部事件压成一个 **Before → Change → After** 模型。

## 5. Speaking · 4 min

**Core topic: How much influence should people have over systems they help build?**

1. Why might workers want to have a say in how technology is designed?
2. What is the difference between privately disagreeing and pushing back against a decision?
3. Why can collective action become difficult to sustain?
4. Should technical expertise automatically give someone more influence over social decisions?
5. Think of a university project: which decisions should be individual, and which should involve the whole team?

第 5 题尽量使用：

**claim → reason → example → limitation**
