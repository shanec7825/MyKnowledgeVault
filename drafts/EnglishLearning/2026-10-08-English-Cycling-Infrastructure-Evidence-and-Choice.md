# 2026-10-08 · Cycling Infrastructure × Evidence and Choice

← [[drafts/EnglishLearning/README|English Learning Hub]]

## Today's Focus

**核心问题：What makes people more willing to cycle in a city?**

今天从昨天的记忆巩固研究转向 **transportation × social choice**。训练两个区分：

- **access ≠ adoption**：有共享单车/车道，不代表所有人都会使用。
- **association ≠ proven causation**：两件事一起变化，不等于已经排除其它解释。

阅读时把信息组织成 **QUESTION → COMPARISON → DATA → RESULT → CAUTION → DESIGN IMPLICATION**，而不是保存一串数字。

## 1. Listening · 6 min

**Source:** [VOA — Bicycles Mean Less Demand for Fuel in East Asia](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/bicycles-mean-less-demand-for-fuel-in-east-asia/4048547.html). 原音约 4 分 19 秒，网页含完整 transcript。

**NEW OPTION (bike-sharing apps) → PEOPLE'S CHOICES → POSSIBLE EFFECT ON FUEL USE**

听完不看原文，说一句：

> Bike-sharing might change ______ because ______.

**Repair (about 1 min).** 只查看 transcript 中第一次丢失关系的位置。判断是声音没听清、指代没跟上、数字打断了理解，还是把 *prediction* 听成了 *confirmed result*。重听附近一句。

**Retrieval (about 40 sec).** 关闭 transcript：报道给出了哪些观察？哪些只是当时的预测？用 **observation vs forecast** 各说一句。

## 2. Reading · 9 min

**Main article:** [University of New Mexico — Study shows protected bike lanes increase bicycle commuting](https://engineering.unm.edu/news/2025/06/study-shows-protected-bike-lanes-increase-bicycle-commuting.html), published 2025-06-06. 短篇研究报道，适合训练文章层级。

**First pass (4 min).** 每读一段，只用一个功能标签，不抄句子：

**QUESTION / FINDING / DATA / COMPARISON / EXPLANATION / IMPLICATION**

**Global model (2 min).** 关掉网页，自行补全：

~~~text
QUESTION       Does the type of bike lane matter?
    ↓
COMPARISON     protected lanes vs ______ lanes vs no new lanes
    ↓
DATA           6 years / 28 US cities / 14,011 ______
    ↓
FINDING        larger increases in ______ near protected facilities
    ↓
INTERPRETATION lower-stress routes may encourage ______
    ↓
CAUTION        observed association ≠ automatic proof of ______
~~~

**Evidence check (2 min).** 把以下三个概念严格区分：

- **What was measured?** Changes in bicycle commuting in geographic areas.
- **What was compared?** Different types of bike-lane infrastructure.
- **What should we NOT conclude?** The study directly proves a precise reduction in accidents, or that every individual cyclist will choose to ride.

原研究报告：安装受保护车道的街区组，其通勤骑行人数**增长幅度**约为普通车道街区组的 **1.8 倍**。注意是 **increase was 1.8× larger**，不是“车道上的总人数是 1.8 倍”。原研究的措辞是 **associated with**，不要无条件改写成 **caused**。

**One-sentence compression (1 min).**

> Compared with ______, ______ was associated with ______, although ______.

**Optional nuance (outside 30 min):** [2026 Berlin streetscape study abstract](https://yonsei.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/perceived-streetscape-quality-and-bike-lane-effectiveness-a-compu/) 指出不同车道形式与骑行行为的关系还受到街道视觉安全感影响。对比两个研究：**context may change what works best**。

## 3. Active Expressions · 4 min

### [[associated-with|be associated with]]

**核心关系：** A and B vary together → relationship observed, mechanism not automatically established.

- be associated with higher ridership
- be associated with a change in behavior
- **不要直接替换成** cause / prove

**中文→英文 retrieval：** “受保护车道与更多骑行通勤的增长有关。”  
> Protected bike lanes were ______ larger increases in bicycle commuting.

### [[lower-the-barrier-to|lower the barrier to]]

**核心关系：** a difficult or intimidating action → becomes easier to start.

- lower the barrier to cycling
- lower the barrier to learning programming
- lower the barrier to participation

**迁移表达（非原文引句）：** Protected routes may lower the barrier to cycling for less-confident riders.

**中文→英文 retrieval：** “把一项活动开始时的门槛降低。”  
> A clear tutorial can ______ learning a new framework.

### Old expression · [[validate|validate]]

回顾昨天的学习检测：**validate a claim against evidence**。今天迁移到城市设计：

> We need better data to validate whether a change in road design improves access.

**Pronunciation / discourse (30 sec within 4 min):**  
> Protected LANES / were asSOCiated with / LARGer INcreases / in BIcycle COMmuting.

读两遍，停顿按意义分组；再不看文本解释 **associated with** 为什么比 **caused** 更谨慎。


### Visual retrieval · See → Hide → Explain

**Concept map (a reasoning aid, not a causal conclusion):**

```text
CITY DESIGN ──→ PERCEIVED SAFETY ──→ WILLINGNESS TO CYCLE
     │                                      │
     └──→ ACCESS TO ROUTES ─────────────────┘
                        ↓
              OBSERVED COMMUTING CHANGE
                        ↑
```

**Look for 20 seconds, hide the diagram, then explain it in English.** Use *be associated with* and *lower the barrier to*. Transfer: replace bike lanes with a university programming club and explain which barriers change. This diagram is a hypothesis map, not proof of causation.

## 4. Speaking · 4 min

**Topic: How should a city decide whether to build more protected bike lanes?**

1. What makes cycling attractive or difficult in a city?
2. How is a protected lane different from a painted lane?
3. What does the study measure, and what does it leave unanswered?
4. Should a city focus only on the number of cyclists? What else matters?
5. Transfer: How might a university **lower the barrier to** joining a programming club?

至少在最后两题使用一次 **be associated with** 或 **lower the barrier to**，并加入一个限制条件（*however / although*）。
