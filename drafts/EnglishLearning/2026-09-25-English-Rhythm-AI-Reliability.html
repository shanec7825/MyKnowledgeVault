Matching text for the SYNTHESIZED study summary

A plausible output from artificial intelligence is not necessarily a safe output. In a factory, a robot path must avoid collisions rather than merely look efficient. MIT researchers developed a method called HardFlow to address strict requirements like these. It allows the model more freedom while exploring possible answers and then enforces hard constraints on the final result. Experiments suggest that this approach can improve the quality of valid solutions. The findings are promising, but no laboratory experiment guarantees that every deployed system will be safe.
