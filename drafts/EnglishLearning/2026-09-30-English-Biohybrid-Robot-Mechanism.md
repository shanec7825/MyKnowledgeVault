## 1. Extensive Listening · 6 min

**Topic → What happened → Why it matters**

判断：
> local comprehension ✓ / global model ?

## 2. Intensive Reading · 11 min

**Source:** [MIT News — Powered by muscle cells, a paper-thin robot swims through watery maze](https://news.mit.edu/2026/powered-by-muscle-cells-paper-thin-robot-swims-through-watery-maze-0929)

### Article model

```text
DESIGN
thin gel + living muscle cells
        ↓
STIMULUS
light
        ↓
BIOLOGICAL RESPONSE
muscle cells twitch
        ↓
PHYSICAL MOVEMENT
fin flaps → robot swims
        ↓
CONTROL
light side / interval → direction & speed
```

**Method:** What did researchers do?  
**Mechanism:** How does the thing actually work?

## 3. Active Expressions

- **in response to**
- **enough to + verb**
- **at regular / various intervals**

Retrieval:
> a server sends data in response to ______.

## 4. Sentence / Cause

> X happens, **causing Y**.

不要把 `causing...` 当新句子；直接画成 **X → Y**。

## 5. Speaking

**What makes a biohybrid robot different from a normal robot?**

1. What makes the aquabot move?
2. How is direction controlled?
3. Why might living muscle be useful?
4. What difficulties may appear when machines contain living cells?
