## 1. Extensive Listening · 7 min

**Source:** [VOA Learning English](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/)

**WHO/WHAT → WHAT HAPPENED → WHY IT MATTERS**

- sound recognition
- vocabulary
- chunking
- retention

## 2. Intensive Reading · 9 min

**Source:** [MIT News — System helps humans predict when self-driving cars will make mistakes](https://news.mit.edu/2026/system-helps-humans-predict-when-self-driving-cars-will-make-mistakes-0902)

### Article model

```text
PROBLEM
AI driving decisions can be opaque
        ↓
WHY IT MATTERS
humans may need to anticipate mistakes
        ↓
METHOD
CW-Net maps internal reasoning
to understandable concepts
        ↓
GOAL / RESULT
better prediction, troubleshooting and trust
```

## 3. Active Expressions

- **anticipate** — expect or prepare for something before it happens.
- **underlying** — below the surface; the deeper mechanism.
- **translate A into B** — convert one representation into another.

Retrieval:
> I understand how to use ___, but I don't fully understand the underlying ___.

## 4. Speaking

**Should we trust an AI if we cannot understand how it made its decision?**

1. Why are some AI systems difficult to understand?
2. Why does explainability matter more in driving than movie recommendations?
3. Is a correct answer without an explanation always a problem?
4. Where else does understanding the underlying process matter?

## 5. Chunking

> A self-driving car / may make the right decision / without showing us / how it reached that decision.

保持意义块，而不是保存每个词。

## Reflection

> Breakdown: ______.  
> Output: I could / couldn't explain it without the text.
