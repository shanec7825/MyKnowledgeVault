**make room for (something/someone)** — allow space, attention, or opportunity for something new or different.

- make room for new interpretations
- make room for different voices
- make room for students to ask questions

**Retrieval cue:** “在已有体系中为新观点/参与者留出空间。” → ______

**Transfer:** Rather than replacing every old method, a course can make room for new approaches.
