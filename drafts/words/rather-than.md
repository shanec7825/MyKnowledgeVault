**rather than**: used to contrast two options.

Example: Rather than memorize sentences, explain the main idea.

Retrieval: 与其……不如…… → ______

Rather than worrying about the final score, refocus attention on the next action.

Retrieval: “与其反复担心考试分数，不如先检查题目条件。” → ______ worrying about the grade, check the question's conditions first.
