## Speaking · 4 min

**Should traditions stay unchanged to remain authentic?**

1. What everyday practice reminds you of home?
2. Why do people preserve familiar traditions?
3. What might change when a tradition travels?
4. Can a new version still respect its origins?
5. Should a university preserve old teaching methods or make room for new ones?

Use one new expression, one example, and one limitation.
