Active language · 4 min

cut into ...

Meaning: reduce the time available for something. Retrieve: “熬夜调试占用了我的睡眠时间。” → Late-night debugging ___ ___ my sleep time.

Pattern: cut into + time / budget / sleep. Transfer to a sports or music practice schedule.

be associated with ...

Meaning: connected in data, not necessarily caused. Retrieve: “睡眠时间与考试成绩有关联。” → Sleep duration was ___ ___ exam results.

Pattern: X is associated with Y ≠ X causes Y. Stress SO in associated.
