Active language · 4 min

jog someone's memory

“一段旋律可能唤起我的记忆。” → A melody might ___ my ___.

Recall from meaning first. Then apply it to remembering a command-line shortcut.

be more likely to

“唱歌的学生可能更容易回忆完整短语。” → Students who sing may be ___ ___ ___ recall the phrase.

A probability claim is not a certainty. Try it in a different subject.

Review: rather than

“与其只认出，不如自己说出来。” → ___ ___ only recognize it, try producing it yourself.

Pronunciation and retrieval

Stress JOG and MEM in jog my memory. In more LIKELY to, keep the infinitive to + verb. Combine rather than with be more likely to in an original sentence about studying.

Speaking · 4 min
