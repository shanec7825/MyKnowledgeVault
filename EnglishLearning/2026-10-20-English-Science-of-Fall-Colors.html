Reading questions

  2. Does less green pigment mean all yellow pigment has just been created?

  4. Which source explains the mechanism and which reports historical examples?
  5. Can a single late autumn prove a long-term global trend?
Optional synthesis, after your own attempt

Shorter days reduce the dominance of green chlorophyll in many deciduous leaves. Other pigments become visible, while species and environmental conditions help explain variation. An observed seasonal difference is not automatically evidence of a particular long-term cause.
