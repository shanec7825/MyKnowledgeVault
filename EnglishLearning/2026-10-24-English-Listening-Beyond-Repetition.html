Repair hint (only after trying)

Retrieval changes the act of remembering; interleaving changes topic order; spacing changes the interval. A difficult recall attempt can expose a precise gap.

Active language · 4 min

pinpoint a gap in …

Meaning cue: 准确找出理解中的缺口 → “___ a ___ in my understanding.”

Use it to describe an exact misunderstanding in a programming lecture. Stress PIN-point.

test yourself on …

Meaning cue: 在某主题上自测 → “___ yourself ___ the article's structure.”

Transfer to data structures. Contrast test yourself on a topic with test yourself with questions.

rather than · review

Meaning cue: 与其死记录音，不如重建论证 → “___ memorize the recording, rebuild the argument.”

Now compare rereading notes with explaining an algorithm from memory.
