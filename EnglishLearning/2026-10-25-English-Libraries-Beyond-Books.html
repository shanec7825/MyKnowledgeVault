Public Library Services for Strong Communities: 2025 Survey

Public Library Association / American Library Association · June 23, 2026

Read the original English report announcement →

In the 2025 survey, libraries reported services beyond lending books. The published findings include 56% offering food-security support, 44.7% serving as cooling or warming centers, and 98.5% having at least one external partnership. The survey received responses from 1,896 libraries (a 20.6% response rate), with weighted results. These figures describe reported services, not proof that any service caused better outcomes.

Active language 4 minutes

respond to a need

Meaning → English: “回应社区的实际需求” → Libraries ___ ___ a community ___.

Libraries can respond to a need for digital access. Now transfer it to a student software project.

serve as a hub for

Meaning → English: “成为学习与交流的中心” → A library can ___ ___ a ___ ___ learning.

A library may serve as a hub for local learning. Transfer it to a campus club.

bridge the gap · review

Meaning → English: “缩小数字技能差距” → ___ the ___ in digital skills.

Pronunciation, syntax & transfer check

reSPOND to + a need; serve as + a hub for + noun/-ing; bridge the gap means reduce, not necessarily erase, a difference. Close this guide and combine two expressions in an unrelated music or sports example.
