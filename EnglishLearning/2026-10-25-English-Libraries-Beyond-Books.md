## Reading · 9 min

**Material:** [ALA / PLA 2025 services survey press release](https://www.ala.org/news/2026/06/new-pla-data-reveals-public-libraries-boosted-support-food-security-health-and). Read the **opening, key findings, and survey-method paragraph**; skip the organization biography. Distinguish **2025 data** from the **2026 release date**.

1. **Skim & label (3 min):** Assign functions to sections: **claim about library roles → survey evidence → concrete service examples → method / scope → cautious inference**. Note which sentences report data and which advocate for libraries.
2. **Local sentence (2 min):** Compare these reported survey results: **56%** offered food-security support; **44.7%** served as cooling/warming centers; **98.5%** had at least one partnership. Explain what the percentages describe, and why they **do not** mean 56% of people received food.
3. **Scope check (2 min):** The release reports **1,896 responding libraries**, a **20.6% response rate**, and **weighted results**. Does that prove a new service caused better outcomes? What uncertainty remains?
4. **Close-book reconstruction (2 min):** Fill in: **changing community needs → libraries offer ___ → survey measures ___ → sample/method limits ___ → my cautious conclusion ___**. Write a **25–35-word English model**, not five translated sentences.

<details><summary>Repair hint — only after your first attempt</summary>

A survey describes reported services and patterns; it does not by itself demonstrate that those services caused improvements. Percentages refer to libraries in the reported analysis, not the share of residents helped.

</details>

## Active Expressions · 4 min

### [[words/respond-to-a-need|respond to a need]]

- **Retrieve from meaning:** “图书馆回应社区的实际需求” → Libraries ___ ___ a real community ___.
- **Use:** Libraries can **respond to a need** for digital access.
- **Transfer:** How might your student software project respond to a need on campus?
- **Pronunciation / syntax:** *respond to + a need / changing needs*; stress **SPOND**; link “respond-to-a” naturally.

### [[words/serve-as-a-hub-for|serve as a hub for]]

- **Retrieve from meaning:** “图书馆成为学习与交流的中心” → The library can ___ ___ a ___ ___ learning and conversation.
- **Use:** A library may **serve as a hub for** local learning activities.
- **Transfer:** Could a campus club serve as a hub for student projects?
- **Syntax:** *serve as + noun*; *a hub for + noun / -ing*. Stress **HUB**.

### [[words/bridge-the-gap|bridge the gap]] · review

- **Retrieve from meaning:** “帮助缩小数字技能差距” → help ___ the ___ in digital skills.
- **Contrast:** **bridge the gap** means reduce a difference, not necessarily erase it.
- **Transfer:** Say how an introductory programming workshop might bridge the gap between beginners and experienced students.

**Closed-book check:** Retrieve all three from Chinese cues; combine two in a **new** example about sports, music, or an AI club. Record the first hesitation.
