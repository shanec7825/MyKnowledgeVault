# 2026-09-27 · REM Sleep × Evidence and Interpretation

← [[EnglishLearning/README|English Learning Hub]]

## Today's Focus

**区分 observation / evidence 与 interpretation。**

## 1. Extensive Listening · 7 min

**Source:** [VOA Learning English](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/)

每听完一个意义块，用 2–4 个词压缩；只允许最后留下 4 个关键词再复述。

重点检查：
> I understood the sentence, but did I lose the previous idea?

## 2. Intensive Reading · 10 min

**Source:** [ScienceDaily — REM sleep paradox](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260925093206.htm)

### Article model

```text
KNOWN FACT
brain needs substantial energy
        ↓
QUESTION
what happens during REM sleep?
        ↓
MEASUREMENT
blood supply + ATP / metabolic signals
        ↓
UNEXPECTED RESULT
blood supply ↑ while ATP ↓
        ↓
POSSIBLE EXPLANATION
consumption may exceed replenishment
```

关键：小鼠研究不能直接当作“已证明人类做梦会耗尽能量”。

## 3. Active Expressions

- **suggest that** — indicate a possible interpretation, not necessarily prove.
- **faster than ... can ...**
- **replenish** — restore a supply that has been used.

Retrieval:
> Information arrives faster than I can ______.

## 4. Speaking

**If the brain is highly active during sleep, what does “rest” mean?**

1. What happens to energy during REM sleep in the study?
2. Why is the result a paradox?
3. Does high activity mean sleep is not restful?
4. Why be careful when applying mice findings to humans?
5. What else can look inactive outside but busy inside?

## 5. Reading Rule

看到：
- **found / observed** → result
- **may suggest / could mean** → interpretation

不要把两者合并。
