## Training Method

**Listening:** follow the overall message and repair one exact breakdown.  
**Reading:** identify paragraph function and reconstruct a global model.  
**Active language:** retrieve two or three expressions from meaning, then transfer.  
**Speaking:** explain an idea with a concrete example and limitation.  
**Writing:** produce an original 80–120-word response and preserve evidence.  
**Review:** reconstruct without looking.
