Reading 9 minutes

Introduction to Web Accessibility

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative · updated February 3, 2026

Read the original English guide →

Accessible digital design aims to remove barriers that stop people from perceiving, understanding, navigating, or interacting with a website. This benefits people with disabilities and can help users facing temporary or situational limits. W3C also distinguishes automated accessibility checks from real-world human evaluation.

Active language 4 minutes

make ... accessible to ...

Meaning → English: “让课程资料对更多学生可获取” → Make course materials ___ ___ more students.

Captions make video lessons accessible to more learners. Now transfer it to software documentation.

remove barriers to ...

Meaning → English: “消除参与讨论的障碍” → remove ___ ___ participation.

Keyboard controls remove barriers to using an interface. Transfer it to your own student bot.

bridge the gap · review

Meaning → English: “缩小新手与熟练开发者之间的差距” → ___ the ___ between beginners and experienced developers.

Syntax, pronunciation, transfer

ac-CESS-i-ble (stress CESS); accessible to + people; barriers to + noun / -ing (not “to learn”). Close this guide; retrieve all three and use two in an unrelated sports or music example. Bridge means reduce a gap, not necessarily eliminate it.
