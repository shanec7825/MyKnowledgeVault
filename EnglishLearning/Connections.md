## 2026-10-30 · Singing and Recall

- [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-30-English-Singing-and-Recall|2026-10-30 · Singing and Recall]]: **VOA 2015: familiar tune → memory question → three-group Hungarian experiment → reported advantage → classroom example**; **Ludke et al. 2014 original study: randomized conditions → 15-minute phrase learning → five tests → stronger spoken verbatim recall → limits**.
- **Evidence boundary:** The singing group had the highest performance on **four of five** measures, but between-group differences were **statistically significant on the two spoken verbatim-recall tests**. Sixty adults and short-term phrase learning do **not** establish improved general fluency, long-term retention, or benefits for all learners. Distinguish the VOA's classroom illustration from the randomized experiment.
- **Cross-day:** [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-24-English-Listening-Beyond-Repetition|Listening Beyond Repetition]] distinguishes recognition from retrieval; today the strongest evidence is on **spoken recall**. [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-22-English-Music-Across-Cultures|Music Across Cultures]] connects music to communication, while today it acts as a possible **retrieval cue**. [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-29-English-Beyond-the-Box-Score|Beyond the Box Score]] asks which actions a measurement captures; today asks which outcomes show a statistically supported difference.
- **Active retrieval:** [[words/jog-someones-memory|jog someone's memory]], [[words/be-more-likely-to|be more likely to]], review [[drafts/words/rather-than|rather than]]. Transfer to recalling a command-line shortcut **versus** explaining what the command does.

## 2026-10-29 · Beyond the Box Score

- [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-29-English-Beyond-the-Box-Score|2026-10-29 · Beyond the Box Score]]: **VOA (2018) Bill Russell interview: recognition problem → team-winning standard → off-ball work → adjust to actual teammates**; **NBA (2025) Hustle Award report: invisible efforts → observable categories → per-minute comparison → interpretation and limits**.
- **Evidence boundary:** Russell's time-on-the-ball estimate is a personal illustration, **not a universal verified measurement**. NBA hustle categories record some actions; a per-minute rate adjusts for playing time, **not for every context or causal factor**. High hustle numbers alone do not prove an individual caused a victory.

## 2026-10-28 · Roots Below Ground

- [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-28-English-Roots-Below-Ground|2026-10-28 · Roots Below Ground]]: **VOA (2015) report: invisible roots → exhibition → root functions → soybean nodule example**; **USDA ARS: limited usable nitrogen → atmospheric nitrogen's abundance → microbial exception → legume nodule mechanism**; **US Botanic Garden (2015 archive): hidden importance → exhibit methods → soil conservation**.
- **Scope repair:** atmospheric nitrogen is about **78% of air**, not a form all plants can directly use. Roots absorb water and minerals; rhizobia in **certain legume root nodules** convert nitrogen gas into usable compounds. An exhibit is public science communication, not a causal experiment.
- **Cross-day:** [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-27-English-Sleep-and-Study|Sleep and Study]] distinguished survey association from causation; today compare a mechanistic explainer with an exhibition narrative. [[EnglishLearning/2026-10-20-English-Science-of-Fall-Colors|Fall Colors]] likewise moves from visible change to hidden biological process.
